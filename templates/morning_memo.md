# Morning Memo Template

> The agent fills this template via the `write_morning_memo` tool. Hard cap: 200 words for the body. Charts are inserted as React components — pass chart specs through, render in the UI layer.

---

## Template (Markdown)

```markdown
# Well {WELL_ID} — Triage Memo
*Generated {DATE} from {N_DDRS} DDRs*

## Headline
{ONE_LINE_PUNCHLINE}

## At a Glance
- **Total days:** {ACTUAL_DAYS} actual / {PLANNED_DAYS} plan ({DELTA_SIGNED})
- **TD reached:** {TD_MD} m MD / {TD_TVD} m TVD
- **NPT:** {NPT_PERCENT}% ({NPT_DAYS} days lost)
- **On-bottom drilling:** {ON_BOTTOM_PERCENT}% of total

## Days vs. Depth
{CHART:days_vs_depth}

## NPT Breakdown
{CHART:npt_pareto}

Top three NPT drivers:
1. **{NPT_CAT_1}** — {NPT_DAYS_1} days ({NPT_PCT_1}% of NPT) — {ROOT_CAUSE_1}
2. **{NPT_CAT_2}** — {NPT_DAYS_2} days ({NPT_PCT_2}% of NPT) — {ROOT_CAUSE_2}
3. **{NPT_CAT_3}** — {NPT_DAYS_3} days ({NPT_PCT_3}% of NPT) — {ROOT_CAUSE_3}

## Notable Events
- **Day {DAY_A}:** {EVENT_A_DESC} ({DEPTH_A} m, {DURATION_A})
- **Day {DAY_B}:** {EVENT_B_DESC} ({DEPTH_B} m, {DURATION_B})
- **Day {DAY_C}:** {EVENT_C_DESC} ({DEPTH_C} m, {DURATION_C})

## Recommendation for next spud
{RECOMMENDATION}

---
*Sources: {SOURCE_FILES} | Generated by DDR Triage Agent*
```

## Worked example (for the system prompt as a few-shot)

Use this as an example of the *voice* the agent should write in. Keep tight.

```markdown
# Well 15/9-F-14 — Triage Memo
*Generated 2026-05-10 from 31 DDRs*

## Headline
Well took 27 days vs. 19-day plan. Δ +8.0 days driven by Day 12 BHA twist-off and a 3.4-day fishing recovery.

## At a Glance
- **Total days:** 27.0 actual / 19.0 plan (+8.0)
- **TD reached:** 4,012 m MD / 3,847 m TVD
- **NPT:** 22.3% (6.0 days lost)
- **On-bottom drilling:** 41.8% of total

## Days vs. Depth
{CHART:days_vs_depth}

## NPT Breakdown
{CHART:npt_pareto}

Top three NPT drivers:
1. **fishing** — 3.4 days (56% of NPT) — Twist-off in 8½" section, run 3 BHA
2. **mechanical** — 1.6 days (27% of NPT) — Top drive failure on Day 18, replacement parts wait
3. **lost_circulation** — 1.0 days (17% of NPT) — Partial losses at 3,247 m, treated with LCM

## Notable Events
- **Day 12:** BHA twist-off at 2,847 m MD (8½" section, 22 hrs to recover fish)
- **Day 18:** Top drive failure (38 hrs WOR including replacement)
- **Day 23:** Partial losses at 3,247 m, treated with LCM (26 hrs)

## Recommendation for next spud
Pull and inspect Run 3 BHA every 8 days based on twist-off failure mode. Consider PDC bit type with reinforced shoulder for the 8½" section based on offset ROP comparison.

---
*Sources: 15/9-F-14_DDR_001.xml through 15/9-F-14_DDR_031.xml | Generated by DDR Triage Agent*
```

---

## Notes for the agent

- **Punchline first.** The headline must be readable in 5 seconds.
- **Numbers are concrete.** "27 days" not "approximately a month." "Δ +8.0 days" not "significant overrun."
- **Events are anchored.** Every notable event has a day number AND a depth.
- **The recommendation is specific.** "Pull Run 3 BHA every 8 days" not "improve BHA inspection cadence."
- **Citations at the bottom.** This is what makes a YP trust the output.
